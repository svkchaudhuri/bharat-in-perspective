READING THE DATA

Development has more than one dimension.

A larger economy does not automatically mean longer lives, safer food or equal opportunity. Compare individual indicators and their histories. This dashboard does not combine them into an arbitrary league table.

COVERAGE NOTE

Food security ≠ food safety.

Nutrition, food access and WHO food-safety capacity are connected. Capacity is self-reported preparedness. Foodborne disease burden and national recalls remain separate coverage gaps.

BEYOND THE AVERAGE

Who shares in prosperity?

GDP per person measures output per person. Household survey distributions show how income or consumption is shared. They are complementary measures, not interchangeable versions of GDP.

HDRO · HUMAN DEVELOPMENT IN CONTEXT

What inequality takes away

These subscription API measures compare the selected economy using the same snapshot. Explore each series for country comparisons, history and source notes. The API does not identify a report edition; the separately labelled HDR 2025 series remain available. Gender parity is a ratio close to 1, not a higher-is-always-better ranking. [2]

A normalised view of the divide

The bottom 40% mean relative to the national mean compares average welfare in the lower-income group with the whole population. 100% means parity with the national mean. It is not a GDP growth rate. A positive shared prosperity premium means the bottom 40% grew faster than the national mean, but both growth rates should also be checked.

Comparability warning: India's WDI household-distribution observations are consumption-based. Some comparators use income. Surveys may miss top incomes and wealth. Gini and Palma do not measure the wealth divide, and a falling Gini alone does not prove rising living standards.

Calculated ratios use matching country-years. Growth premiums additionally require matching survey periods and welfare types. World Bank PIP [10]

LIVED EXPERIENCE & ENVIRONMENT

Wellbeing, cleanliness and carbon.

Happiness is reported life evaluation. Cleanliness is represented by specific air, water and sanitation measures. CO2 emissions per person measure a footprint, not carbon neutrality.

What these numbers can establish

Happiness observations are three-year survey averages, labelled by the ending year; uncertainty intervals are retained in the explorer. [7] Emissions here are territorial CO2 excluding land use, land-use change and forestry. They omit consumption-based imported emissions and are insufficient to establish net zero. Clean air, safe water, sanitation and waste management should be assessed separately.

For additional published waste and wastewater indicators, open SDG 6, 11 or 12 in the SDG progress tab. They retain source years and imputation flags. [8]

EVIDENCE & LIMITATIONS

Every number needs a source and a year.

What is connected

World Development Indicators and Worldwide Governance Indicators are retrieved from the World Bank API, including original names and metadata where supplied. [1] Human development measures include the fixed UNDP HDR 2025 edition and separately named HDRO subscription API series. API credentials stay outside the browser. The API snapshot does not identify a publication edition. [2] Food-safety capacity uses the WHO SPAR second edition only. [3] The live event panel uses the USGS feed. [4] The separation between long-term indicators and event context is inspired by World Monitor. [5]

Compare like with like

  • Latest available can mix observation years. Every value carries its year and age.
  • Latest common year uses the newest year with data for every selected country, calculated separately for each indicator. If there is no common year, the dashboard shows no comparable values.
  • Fixed year uses only that year's observations. Missing data is never filled with zero or interpolated.
  • GDP per capita uses constant 2021 international dollars at purchasing power parity. Growth is real growth; inflation is not automatically classified as good or bad.
  • Gross enrolment can exceed 100%. Poverty thresholds, survey methods, rolling averages and governance estimates need the original definitions. Governance estimates also have uncertainty intervals that this prototype does not plot.
  • Data age is the current calendar year minus the observation year, not a judgement about statistical validity. Figures more than three years old are flagged.
  • No weighted Nordic average, composite development score, or causal claims are produced.

Food safety coverage

Food security, child nutrition and water services are related to wellbeing, but do not measure foodborne hazards directly. WHO food-safety capacity is connected, but measures self-reported preparedness rather than contamination or illness incidence. The second-edition series begins in 2021 and is not joined to the first edition. Foodborne disease burden still needs a separate integration. [6] National recall counts depend on surveillance intensity and should not be ranked as if they measure the same underlying risk.

Refresh and availability

The annual-data snapshot is refreshed with the project's refresh command. Reopening the page does not refetch the World Bank. A failed source retains its previous observations with a cached label, or shows unavailable if no observations exist. Live events refresh while this page is open; the local server caches them for five minutes. No background monitoring runs after you close the application.
